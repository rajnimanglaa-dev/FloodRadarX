The Crisis: Why Traditional Flood Management Fails
01. Delayed Public Warning Systems
Conventional alerts lag 3 to 5 hours behind actual flood cresting, leaving families trapped in low-lying sectors without evacuation time.
02. Siloed Data & Fragmented Tools
Precipitation, dam release gauges, and soil saturation reside in isolated portals with no unified real-time inundation simulation engine.
03. Blind Rescue Dispatch Navigation
First responders navigate without live knowledge of submerged streets, leading to rescue boat delays and stranded convoys.
04. Shelter Allocation Disconnect
Evacuees crowd random buildings while verified relief camps with clean water, medicine, and power remain under-utilized.
